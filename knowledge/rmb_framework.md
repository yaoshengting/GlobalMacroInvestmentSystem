# RMB Framework

## Current View
The RMB is managed for stability with reference to growth, rate differentials, capital flows, trade competitiveness, and policy signaling.
## Confidence
Medium.
## Core Logic
Fundamentals + dollar cycle + flows → market pressure; fixing, state-bank behavior, and macroprudential policy → managed path.
## Supporting Evidence
Official fixing, CFETS basket, reserves, settlement data, trade balance, and onshore/offshore spreads.
## Contrary Evidence
Unobserved flows and global dollar shocks reduce near-term forecastability.
## Falsification Conditions
An explicit regime change or persistent one-way movement without stabilizing policy response.
## Asset Implications
Currency changes affect China returns, exporters/importers, dollar assets, and hedging costs.
## Investment Execution
Use bands and scenarios; never infer intent from one fixing or session.
