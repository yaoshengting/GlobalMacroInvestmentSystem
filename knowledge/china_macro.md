# China Macro

## Current View
The economy is two-speed: advanced manufacturing and exports are relatively resilient while property, consumption, and traditional private credit remain softer.
## Confidence
Medium.
## Core Logic
Industrial upgrading + export capacity → manufacturing resilience; property deleveraging → weaker wealth effect → cautious households and private borrowers.
## Supporting Evidence
Track official production, trade, PMI, social-financing composition, property sales, and household income/consumption data.
## Contrary Evidence
External restrictions may weaken exports; stronger fiscal transfers or property stabilization could broaden demand.
## Falsification Conditions
Sustained broad private-demand acceleration or simultaneous contraction in advanced manufacturing and exports.
## Asset Implications
Separate policy-supported industries from broad-beta claims; retain rate and currency sensitivity analysis.
## Investment Execution
Size China exposure conservatively and require multiple primary indicators before changing allocation.
