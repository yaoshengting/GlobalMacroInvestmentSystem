# Middle East Energy

## Current View
Escalation risk transmits primarily through physical supply, shipping insurance, oil, inflation expectations, yields, and risk premia.
## Confidence
Medium; event probabilities are low-precision.
## Core Logic
Conflict → supply/shipping disruption → oil price → inflation expectations → rates and margins → cross-asset volatility.
## Supporting Evidence
Official notices, verified physical-flow data, shipping conditions, inventories, and futures curves.
## Contrary Evidence
Spare capacity, rerouting, demand weakness, and rapid diplomacy can cap price effects.
## Falsification Conditions
Repeated severe escalation without physical disruption, curve tightening, or inflation repricing.
## Asset Implications
Potential support for cash, gold, and selective energy; headwind for duration-sensitive assets if yields rise.
## Investment Execution
Do not chase oil; preserve liquidity and rebalance only after checking physical evidence.
