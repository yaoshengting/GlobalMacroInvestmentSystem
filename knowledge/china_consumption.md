# China Consumption

## Current View
Consumption is strategically important, but a durable rebalancing requires household income, confidence, services, and social protection—not only goods subsidies.
## Confidence
Medium.
## Core Logic
Income + wealth + safety net + confidence → propensity to consume → services and goods demand → private investment.
## Supporting Evidence
Track disposable income, savings, retail/services, employment, fiscal transfers, and consumer surveys.
## Contrary Evidence
Targeted programs may produce stronger multipliers than expected; weak property wealth may persist.
## Falsification Conditions
Consumption persistently leads nominal growth without broader household-income or transfer support.
## Asset Implications
Prefer companies with evidenced demand and cash flow over thematic labels.
## Investment Execution
Wait for repeated household-level confirmation before changing strategic weights.
