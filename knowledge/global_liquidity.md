# Global Liquidity

## Current View
Liquidity is multi-dimensional: central-bank balance sheets, fiscal cash, bank credit, dollar funding, and private risk appetite must be assessed separately.
## Confidence
Medium-low because measurement and lags vary.
## Core Logic
Policy/fiscal flows → funding conditions → leverage and risk appetite → asset prices, with regime-specific lags.
## Supporting Evidence
Official balance sheets, reserve data, funding spreads, credit growth, and cross-currency bases.
## Contrary Evidence
Productivity or earnings can lift assets despite tight liquidity; weak demand can mute abundant reserves.
## Falsification Conditions
A claimed liquidity measure repeatedly fails out-of-sample across assets and regimes.
## Asset Implications
Use liquidity as context, never as a standalone timing signal.
## Investment Execution
Triangulate at least three channels before adjusting risk.
