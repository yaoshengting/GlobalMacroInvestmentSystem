# Style Rotation

## Current View
Growth and value are complementary sleeves; relative performance depends on earnings revisions, rates, credit, and valuation rather than permanent labels.
## Confidence
Medium.
## Core Logic
Earnings duration + discount rates + credit cycle + valuation spread → relative performance; combination → portfolio diversification.
## Supporting Evidence
Factor returns, earnings revisions, valuation spreads, yield curve, and sector balance sheets.
## Contrary Evidence
Concentration and correlation shifts can erase diversification; cheap sectors can be value traps.
## Falsification Conditions
Persistent diversification failure or structural earnings impairment in either sleeve.
## Asset Implications
Technology supports structural growth; financials, healthcare, dividends, and cash may moderate volatility.
## Investment Execution
Rebalance by target bands and fundamentals, not headline narratives.
