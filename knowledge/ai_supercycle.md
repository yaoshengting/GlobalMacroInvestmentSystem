# AI Supercycle

## Current View
AI capital spending remains in expansion, but returns should increasingly separate infrastructure bottlenecks and monetizers from undifferentiated exposure.
## Confidence
Medium-high; the direction is clearer than the duration or winners.
## Core Logic
Model capability → adoption → compute and power demand → capital expenditure → revenue productivity → free cash flow and return-on-capital screening.
## Supporting Evidence
Public capex guidance, accelerator demand, data-center buildouts, and power/cooling constraints; each release requires dated primary-source verification.
## Contrary Evidence
High valuations, supply normalization, uncertain application monetization, and depreciation costs may compress returns.
## Falsification Conditions
Broad multi-quarter capex cuts, falling utilization, or persistent returns below cost of capital.
## Asset Implications
Favor diversified exposure and balance-sheet quality; distinguish enablers, adopters, and speculative proxies.
## Investment Execution
Use staged allocation and predetermined drawdown reviews; never chase one-day headlines.
