# China Real Estate

## Current View
Property remains a balance-sheet and confidence drag whose stabilization matters more than a return to the former growth model.
## Confidence
Medium.
## Core Logic
Sales → developer cash flow → construction/land demand → local finance and household wealth → credit and consumption.
## Supporting Evidence
Official sales, starts, completions, prices, land transactions, and developer disclosures.
## Contrary Evidence
Inventory programs, financing support, urbanization, or income growth may accelerate stabilization.
## Falsification Conditions
Sustained nationwide sales/price recovery with healthier developer cash flow and no renewed leverage excess.
## Asset Implications
Broad China beta and commodities remain sensitive; avoid extrapolating one-city data.
## Investment Execution
Require breadth and persistence before raising property-linked exposure.
