# US Rates

## Current View
US yields reflect growth, inflation, policy expectations, term premium, and Treasury supply; no single release explains the curve reliably.
## Confidence
Medium.
## Core Logic
Growth/inflation data + Fed reaction function + issuance/term premium → yield curve → discount rates, dollar, credit, and equity multiples.
## Supporting Evidence
Use Federal Reserve communications, Treasury releases, official inflation/labor data, and market pricing with timestamps.
## Contrary Evidence
Safe-haven demand, positioning, and technical flows can overwhelm macro signals temporarily.
## Falsification Conditions
Persistent yield behavior inconsistent with the stated drivers after controlling for policy and supply.
## Asset Implications
Long-duration growth is discount-rate sensitive; cash and bonds provide scenario-dependent ballast.
## Investment Execution
Avoid binary rate calls; use scenarios and review duration at configured thresholds.
