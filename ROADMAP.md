# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation (current)

Typed configuration, source policy, knowledge and playbook templates, daily report generation, CLI, and tests.

## Phase 2 — Evidence pipeline

Add approved public data adapters, timestamps, provenance, caching, normalization, and reproducible snapshots.

## Phase 3 — Decision support

Add hypothesis-change logs, confidence review, scenario scoring, portfolio exposure analytics, and risk dashboards.

## Phase 4 — Review

Add decision journals, attribution, error taxonomy, monthly cognition reviews, and backup/export procedures. Human approval remains mandatory.
