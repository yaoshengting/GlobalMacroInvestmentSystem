# System Architecture

The system turns evidence into disciplined decisions through five layers.

1. **Information Layer** — collect data, preserve source attribution and timestamps, and retain raw inputs.
2. **Knowledge Layer** — maintain long-term frameworks, research views, supporting and contrary evidence, falsifiers, and confidence.
3. **Decision Layer** — judge whether new evidence changes long-term cognition, asset allocation, or risk levels.
4. **Execution Layer** — produce reviewable suggestions; it never trades automatically.
5. **Review Layer** — record outcomes, errors, cognition corrections, and version history.

Financial news is not the final product. The final product is better investment decisions and a lower probability of major error.
