# Fed Cut
## Trigger
An official cut or durable market repricing toward meaningful easing.
## Immediate Checks
Whether the cause is disinflation, recession, liquidity stress, or insurance; inspect real yields and credit.
## Transmission Chain
Easing → yields/dollar/credit → liquidity and demand → asset prices, conditional on cause.
## Portfolio Impact
Compare duration upside with recession-related earnings and credit downside.
## Do
Use cause-specific scenarios and staged rebalancing.
## Do Not
Do not assume every cut is automatically bullish.
## Falsification
Inflation reaccelerates or financial conditions fail to ease.
## Review Window
Same day, one week, and after the next labor/inflation reports.
