# Fed Hike
## Trigger
An official FOMC decision or durable repricing toward materially tighter policy.
## Immediate Checks
Statement, projections, press conference, real yields, curve, dollar, and credit spreads.
## Transmission Chain
Tighter policy → real yields/dollar → financing conditions → demand and valuations.
## Portfolio Impact
Stress-test duration, leverage, banks, bonds, gold, and non-dollar assets.
## Do
Update rate scenarios and assess balance-sheet resilience.
## Do Not
Do not infer the full path from one decision or headline.
## Falsification
Subsequent guidance and data reverse the tightening path.
## Review Window
Same day, next key data release, and next FOMC meeting.
