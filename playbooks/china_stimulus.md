# China Stimulus
## Trigger
Official, funded measures materially change fiscal impulse, household transfers, property support, or credit transmission.
## Immediate Checks
Size, funding, implementation dates, recipients, central/local split, and high-frequency confirmation.
## Transmission Chain
Policy → income/credit/confidence → demand → earnings, imports, currency, and rates.
## Portfolio Impact
Assess China equity breadth, property exposure, commodities, CGBs, and RMB.
## Do
Distinguish announcements from disbursement and measure multipliers.
## Do Not
Do not annualize a headline amount or equate supply support with household demand.
## Falsification
Weak implementation and no persistent response in credit, income, sales, or confidence.
## Review Window
At announcement, monthly data, and quarterly review.
