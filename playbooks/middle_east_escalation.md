# Middle East Escalation
## Trigger
Direct US–Iran conflict escalates, the Strait of Hormuz is impaired, or Brent rises materially with verified physical-risk evidence.
## Immediate Checks
Official confirmation, shipping flows, insurance, inventories, futures curve, dollar, gold, breakevens, and Treasury yields.
## Transmission Chain
Middle East escalation → oil → inflation expectations → Treasury-yield pressure → technology valuation volatility → gold and dollar safe-haven demand.
## Portfolio Impact
Higher risk premium for duration-sensitive assets; possible support for energy, gold, dollar cash, and defensive assets.
## Do
Keep dollar liquidity; continue technology-fund purchases only in stages; reassess gold and defensive allocation.
## Do Not
Do not chase oil, clear long-term technology assets on one day of news, or treat unverified posts as facts.
## Falsification
De-escalation plus normalized physical flows, freight, curve, and inflation pricing.
## Review Window
Every 24 hours during acute escalation, then weekly.
