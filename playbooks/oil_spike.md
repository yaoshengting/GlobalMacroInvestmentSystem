# Oil Spike
## Trigger
Brent crosses a configured threshold with confirmation from the curve or verified physical disruption.
## Immediate Checks
Cause, duration, inventories, curve shape, shipping, spare capacity, and dollar move.
## Transmission Chain
Oil spike → inflation expectations → yields/margins → consumers and risk assets.
## Portfolio Impact
Stress-test duration, transport, consumers, energy, gold, and cash.
## Do
Preserve liquidity; distinguish demand shock from supply shock; rebalance in stages.
## Do Not
Do not chase a one-day move or treat price alone as proof of shortage.
## Falsification
Physical flows normalize and the curve/risk premium reverses.
## Review Window
24 hours, one week, and one month.
