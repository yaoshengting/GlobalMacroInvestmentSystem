# AI Drawdown
## Trigger
QQQ or SOXX reaches a configured drawdown level, or verified earnings/capex evidence breaks the thesis.
## Immediate Checks
Breadth, earnings revisions, capex guidance, utilization, rates, positioning, and liquidity.
## Transmission Chain
Shock → multiple compression/earnings revision → funding and sentiment → suppliers and adopters.
## Portfolio Impact
Separate structural holdings from leveraged or unprofitable proxies.
## Do
Review falsifiers and use predefined staged allocation bands.
## Do Not
Do not buy solely because price fell or liquidate solely on a headline.
## Falsification
Fundamental capex and monetization evidence deteriorate for multiple quarters.
## Review Window
At each drawdown level, earnings cycle, and monthly review.
