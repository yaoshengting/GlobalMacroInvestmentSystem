# USD/CNY Move
## Trigger
USD/CNY reaches a configured level or diverges sharply from fixing and fundamentals.
## Immediate Checks
Dollar index, fixing, onshore/offshore spread, rate differential, flows, trade, and official signals.
## Transmission Chain
Dollar/rate/flow shock → RMB pressure → policy response → earnings translation, hedging, and risk appetite.
## Portfolio Impact
Review unhedged China and dollar exposures and importer/exporter sensitivity.
## Do
Use scenario bands and verify whether the move is dollar-led or China-specific.
## Do Not
Do not infer a regime change from one session.
## Falsification
The move reverses without policy confirmation or sustained flow evidence.
## Review Window
Daily during threshold breach, then weekly.
